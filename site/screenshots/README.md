# Website screenshots

The screenshots on the website show the **real app on demo photos**: the CC0 photos listed below and, since 2026-11-03, twenty-five of the owner's own photos of Prague (Lumix GX9), with the owner's consent. Never anyone else's photos.

1. `python3 -I site/screenshots/fetch_photos.py <folder>` downloads the demo photos listed in [`photos.json`](photos.json) from Wikimedia Commons and checks each one's SHA-256 and licence. They are not kept in the repository.
2. `site/screenshots/build.sh <folder> <work-dir> [best-shot-photo] [owner-folder]` then gathers the photos named in [`demo_set.txt`](demo_set.txt), the CC0 ones from `<folder>` and the owner's from `<owner-folder>`, and:
   - runs the debug app with `FOTOLLECTION_DEMO_FOLDER` and `FOTOLLECTION_DEMO_DUMP` ([`src-tauri/src/demo.rs`](../../src-tauri/src/demo.rs)): it imports the folder with its own settings, cache and project (never the user's), runs the best-shot analysis, records what each IPC command answers and the image protocol's bytes, then quits;
   - shoots the real interface (`ui/dist`) with Playwright ([`shoot.mjs`](shoot.mjs)), answering IPC from that recording, in English and Vietnamese, dark and light: the library, the date stamp, the info frame, the crop editor, best-shot groups, Simple mode (`simple`), Collage mode (`collage` and a sheet's preview `manual-collage-preview`, from the sheets `demo.rs` records with the date stamp and the 3 mm border), postcards twice (`postcard` bordered on the red tram for the landing page, `manual-postcard` and `postcard-night` filled to the edge on trams by night, the sheets and a card's preview, each with "Ahoj Praha!" to the National Museum), and for the manual the filters, the Project block, the print sizes, the frame card, the groups list, the place picker, the Places card and the share card with *Whole photo in a frame* on (`manual-*`; `manual-share-fill`, FR-075), the preview's *This photo* card (`manual-summary`, FR-078) and the postcard text card with *Use for N postcards* and the recent addresses (`manual-postcard-apply`, `manual-postcard-recent`, FR-077; the bridge counts the cards from the recording and offers two public museums' addresses);
   - writes 1600 px WebP screenshots to `site/assets/img/` (shots named `check-*` are only for checking controls by eye and stay out of the site; shots named `manual-*` are only for the manual, placed by the `<!-- shot: … -->` markers in `docs/USER_GUIDE.md` and `USER_GUIDE.vi.md`, [ADR-0031](../../docs/adr/0031-manual-on-the-website-from-the-user-guide.md)).

   - renders every page's link preview (Open Graph, 1200 × 630 JPEG) with [`og.mjs`](og.mjs) from [`og.html`](og.html) and those screenshots: `og-{lang}.jpg` for the home page, `og-{page}-{lang}.jpg` for every other page, in every published language (`site/_data/languages.yml`), with the screenshots of the language the page shows (`assets`). A card's words are `og_card_<page>` and `og_vnlf` in `site/_data/i18n/<code>.yml`; a page names its card with `og:` in its front matter; `scripts/check-site-i18n.mjs` fails when a published language lacks a card's words or image. `node site/screenshots/og.mjs` renders them alone, after a change of wording, with no app and no recording.
   - **the landing page's keynote video** (owner, 2026-11-11; English's for most pages, Vietnamese, Japanese and Korean each its own cut with `--lang`, its places in `GEO` measured on that language's shots; each poster is the cut's first frame, `--frame 0`, through `cwebp -q 80` to `keynote-<cut>[-<lang>]-poster.webp`) with [`keynote.mjs`](keynote.mjs) from [`keynote.html`](keynote.html): 20 s at 30 fps, a desktop cut (1280 × 720) and a phone cut (720 × 900), stepped frame by frame from the screenshots above (never recorded in real time, so each run gives the same video), only the motion added: every photo of the trip, the best ones dated and in colour, two photos on one print, the postcard, every size at dm and Rossmann, Export, the name. A lossless master, then AV1 and H.264 MP4s with no sound, each within 1.5 MB. The headlines and their times are `site/_data/keynote.yml` with the English `kn_*` words, the same the page lays over the video in its own language, so the video ships without them; `--text` bakes them in for review and `--frame <s>` writes one frame. The last frame is the first one, so it loops without a jump. `--lang ja` or `--lang ko` renders the Japanese or Korean page's cut from that language's screenshots and Looks, with the places measured on them (`GEO` in `keynote.html`: the stamp's corner, the two photos of the sheet, each typed row of the はがき or the 우편엽서, the size list); the Export at the end is the gallery's own screenshot, since the open size list covers the panel's foot. `node site/screenshots/keynote.mjs <out-dir> [desktop|mobile] [--text]`; the drafts stay out of the repository.
   - renders the landing page's showcase and the catalog images with [`showcase.mjs`](showcase.mjs) from [`showcase.html`](showcase.html) and those screenshots (never a mock-up): the bento tiles `showcase/{looks,simple,compare,prints,yours,desktops}-{lang}.webp` (1200 × 900, a keynote line over the app), the wide image `showcase/wide-{lang}.webp` (the app over the owner's view of Prague, blurred), and `catalog/{gallery,window,compare,export,collage}-{lang}.jpg` (1600 × 1120, a window with a one-line caption, for the VNLF App Explorer and the README) with `catalog/<name>.jpg` (1600 × 1000, no words, for AppStream). Words that pass their room fail the run. The `look` scenario of the recording (a colour look and its plain preview, `demo.rs`) gives the before and after its real difference.

**Phone crops** (FR-135, UI-VISUAL-003). Each picture of the landing and Features pages also comes as `<name>-phone-<lang>-<theme>.webp`, a 320 × 400 px part of the window (`PHONE` in `shoot.mjs`: the corner of what the picture's caption names, or a box to centre on, the chosen sheet or the stamp's corner of the photo; `estimate` the middle of its zoomed part) written at 640 × 800 and served below 600 px by `shot.html`. `phone()` measures the smallest text of the DOM inside the crop, where it is topmost, as it shows on a 390 px phone (scaled from 320 px to the 328 px carousel card) and fails the run under 11 px; text inside a photo or a rendered sheet is the print and is not measured. The measures go to `phone.json`, merged by `build.sh` into `site/_data/phone.json`, which `shot.html` and `check-site-i18n` read. A `DEMO_DIRS_<LANG>` never points into `<work-dir>`: `build.sh` empties `<work-dir>/demo-src-<lang>` first (LESSONS 2026-11-15).

Photos are chosen by file name in `shoot.mjs` (`PROFILES`: each set's grid photo, the one with GPS, the info frame, the postcard, the straightening, the before and after, the full-bleed card, the folder tabs, the basket and set-aside photos, the export's example name and the recent addresses).

**Each language its own recording** (2026-11-15). `SHOT_LANGS` (en vi by default) names the languages a run shoots; each is recorded in that language (`FOTOLLECTION_DEMO_LANGUAGE`, `demo.rs`), so its postcards are the card that language prints: the European card in English and Vietnamese, a はがき in Japanese, a 우편엽서 in Korean, each sent to a famous place's public address with a demo name in the language (the National Museum in Prague with Emma Johnson or Nguyễn Văn Anh, Tokyo Tower with 山田太郎, the Sejong Center with 김민준; owner, 2026-11-15). A language with its own photos lists them in `demo_set.<lang>.txt` and finds them in `DEMO_DIRS_<LANG>` (folders joined by `:`; HEIC read as the app reads it, on a Mac):

```sh
SHOT_LANGS="ja ko" DEMO_DIRS_JA="<JAPAN DEMO>:<folder with Gokurakuji.jpg>" \
  DEMO_DIRS_KO="<9adox Korea 26>:<imyourctus KOR image>:<fall in seoul 2026>" \
  site/screenshots/build.sh <any-folder> <work-dir> P1057694.JPG
```

The site shows a language's set when `site/_data/languages.yml` gives it `assets: <lang>`; its makeover keeps English's v0.12 gallery (`legacy: en`). The bento tiles, the catalog images and the app's manual pictures come from the English and Vietnamese runs only.

**Measuring the panels.** `MEASURE=1 node site/screenshots/shoot.mjs <dump> <out-dir>` (with `MEASURE_LANG=vi`, `hu` or `en-XA`, the pseudo-locale of a `VITE_PSEUDO=1` build, and `MEASURE_THEME=dark` for the other variants; buttons are found by their i18n keys in every locale, `say()`) shoots nothing for the site: it opens every panel and preview on the recording, prints a table of their widths, gaps and control heights (with the rows of a list, 4 px, and the mode switch's rows, 12 px), saves `measure-*.png` to look at, and fails when one differs from the lego table in [STYLE_RULE §4](../../docs/STYLE_RULE.md#4-uiux). It then walks the gallery, the filters, the export dialog, Settings (General, Export, Updates), Simple, Collage, the photo preview in both layouts and the update window at 1180, 1400 and 1600 px, and fails on any text wider than the room inside its padding (a range over the content against the box, so centred text that spills on both sides is caught), any head, toolbar, button group or dialog footer whose child passes its edge, and any text-only segment off centre by more than 0.5 px (v0.13.0, step 5). It also fails on a folded card taller than its header, in the Pro panel and the preview's Edit, Print and Info tabs (owner, 2026-11-07: a band seen once under a folded card's title). `MEASURE_ENGINE=webkit` runs it all in WebKit, the engine of the app's window on macOS and Linux, instead of Chromium. The `style-audit` skill (`.claude/skills/style-audit/`) runs it for every UI change. Stop `tauri dev` before recording a dump: the running app would take the launch.

**The print-basket preview (`swipe`) and best shot show the owner's cat**, not the demo photos. `uv run python -I site/screenshots/best_shot_photos.py P1057694.JPG <folder>` makes a burst from that one photo (Lumix GX9, by @hoangphi01, all rights reserved, not kept in the repository): the photo itself, a softer, a shaken and a darker copy, one second apart, EXIF kept. `build.sh` records it on its own when given the photo as a third argument, and `shoot.mjs` shoots the basket preview (`swipe`, the sharp shot in the basket) and the best-shot screens (`groups`, `manual-groups`) from that recording, so every other shot keeps the demo photos. The app groups the four and picks the sharp one with its real scores. Needs cargo, npm, `cwebp` and uv (for Python with Pillow).

## The owner's photos

Twenty-five photos of Prague, November 2026, taken by the owner (@hoangphi01) on a Lumix DC-GX9, listed by file name in [`demo_set.txt`](demo_set.txt): trams by night and by day, the city from Letná, the National Museum by night, the metro, the Vltava, autumn, food, the Old Town. **All rights reserved**; the photos are never kept in the repository, only the screenshots that show them. The GX9 records no GPS, so they carry no location; a few show strangers small in a crowd. The grid's chosen photo and the Lumix info frame come from them when they are in the set (`shoot.mjs`). **For the new look (FR-122, owner's consent 2026-11-12):** the owner's whole Prague trip, the night trams graded with a Lightroom preset, may be shown as the demo of the app's warm chrome when it is released (v0.14.1): the gallery of the trip in the dark theme, as the owner's own screenshot of 2026-11-12 shows it; the photos themselves stay out of the repository.

**The landing page's finished prints** (`site/assets/img/results/`, FR-135) are not screenshots either: `build.sh` runs `site/screenshots/results.py <dump> <lang>` after each recording, which copies the app's own renders of the language's postcards, a photo with its date stamp, a two-photo collage sheet and one in its info frame (the photos it names per language, none repeating the postcards' places) to `<name>-<lang>.webp`, and writes to `site/_data/results.json` their sizes, a credit for each (who took it, as `fn_photos` names them, and the camera from its EXIF) and the rows they are laid in by shape, so no corner stays empty.

**The landing page's Looks** (`site/assets/img/looks/`) are not screenshots: `site/screenshots/looks.sh <photo> [lang]` renders one photo, cut to 3:4, through each built-in look with the app's own engine (`crates/core/examples/render_looks.rs`) and writes 1200 × 1600 WebP: `<slug>.webp` from the owner's P1067865 (a Prague tram, with his consent) for every page, `<slug>-ja.webp` from IMG_5710 (a Kamakura street, `JAPAN DEMO`, a HEIC that `sips` turns into a JPEG first) and `<slug>-ko.webp` from @9adox's DSCF1990 (Gwanghwamun) for the pages whose `languages.yml` names them in `looks`, each credited in that page's `fn_photo`. Run it again when a built-in look's recipe changes.
