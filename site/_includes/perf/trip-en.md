### Two budgets missed, and found

On these 20 MP files the first run missed two budgets: export ran at 7.6 photos a second and memory peaked at 2.14 GB, over the 2 GB ceiling. Timing each stage of one photo showed where it went: decoding about 100 ms, cropping 2 ms, and encoding the print 203 ms. The print encoder optimised its tables in a second pass, which kept every coefficient of the image in memory, about 160 MB a photo, six photos at once about 1.5 GB, for files only 0.9 % smaller. With the standard tables it writes the file in strips: 138 ms, the same error against the rendered image, a few kilobytes more.

Part of the memory was not the app's at all. The benchmark made its thumbnails on every core, where the app uses two background threads, and kept its own preview measurements alive through the export; measured the way the app works, 0.35 to 0.39 GB remain before an export starts. Since v0.9.7 the number of photos worked on at once also follows the largest photo of the export (fewer for very large ones), so the peak stays under the ceiling at any size: 10.1 photos a second and 1.31 GB for this folder.
